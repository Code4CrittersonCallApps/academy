How every course works

1 · CLIFF NOTES

Read the tile

Every module starts with 3–4 bullets: the whole idea in 30 seconds.

2 · DRILL DOWN

Open the lessons

3–5 short lessons per module, with examples you can copy.

3 · DO IT

One hands-on exercise

Build or say something real, with a clear "done when".

4 · CHECK

Quick quiz

Four questions with explanations. Pass and the module is marked done on your device.
